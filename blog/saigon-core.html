Saigon core

September 13, 2026

Dhrumil’s Toronto Core made me want to write my own Saigon list. I’ve been back since 2020, so I think it’s overdue. Every district has its own vibe and somehow they all add up to a uniquely Saigon one, this list is my attempt at pinning that down. (I realize that about half of these are food, and that’s exactly why I love this city.)

  1. Cơm tấm sườn chả (broken rice with grilled pork chop and egg meatloaf. If my stomach had extra space I’d throw in a fried egg on top)
  2. Endless coffee shops
  3. Phở Lệ (my family and I have been going since pretty much the day I was born)
  4. Japantown in the heart of district 1
  5. Chaotic traffic + motorbikes that terrify anyone crossing the street here for the first time
  6. Ốc (snail) dishes
  7. Sidewalk red and blue plastic stools
  8. Alleyways
  9. Hủ tiếu, a Southern noodle soup, of any kind (or best yet: hủ tiếu gõ from a cart at 11 PM, the kind a vendor announces by knocking two sticks together)
  10. Xôi mặn (savory sticky rice, a breakfast staple in my kindergarten days)
  11. Old French colonial buildings
  12. An Đông market in district 5 (my mom did most of my back-to-school shopping here when I was in secondary school, and honestly it beats Bến Thành)
  13. Chè (sweet soup desserts, my favorite is water chestnut and egg sweet soup which is actually a Chinese dish)
  14. Milk tea culture (not distinctly Saigon, I know, but I like that there are many options to try)
  15. Rain that is sometimes relentless, but helps make the city cool on hot days
  16. Convenience stores at 2 AM
  17. Cà phê sữa đá (iced coffee with condensed milk. I now prefer mine unsweetened but still enjoy one once in a while)
  18. Old apartment buildings turned into cafés and shops
  19. Thảo Điền (more gentrified and overpriced every year, and I still show up whenever a new restaurant or café opens)
  20. Bình Thạnh district (I stayed here when I first moved back, 10 minutes to district 2 or district 1 without the price tags of either back then)
  21. Bún bò Huế (spicy beef noodle soup from Huế, ironically much better here)
  22. Fresh coconuts and sugarcane juice on the street
  23. Early mornings where people drink coffee and eat breakfast on sidewalks
  24. Bánh bao Sài Gòn (steamed pork buns, different flavor profiles from what you get in, say, Hanoi)
  25. Bò kho (beef stew) with phở noodles instead of bánh mì because I’m a sucker for noodles
  26. Busy traffic circles where five or six streets converge
  27. Bitexco Tower (or the Avengers Tower as people say)
  28. Thảo Cầm Viên/Saigon Zoo (the first apartment I stayed in when I moved back looked out over it, and my grandpa used to take me there when I was little)
